A Graph opens in a product.

`/u/<user>/<graph>` now opens the product you last had open on a Graph, or Explorer the first
time. Any `?right=`, `?page=` or `?onboarding=` on the link comes along. The launcher no longer
lists *Studio*, and the graph page's own left rail (Info · Explorer · Runs · Events) is gone. Runs
and Events are tabs of Activity, which the header's bell opens, and Info is a tab of the
Modeller's Graph settings. Old `?panel=` links still land on their paths.
