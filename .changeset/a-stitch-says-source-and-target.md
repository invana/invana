The declare-a-stitch dialog says Source and Target (stitch-models.md ST28).

The two pickers were labelled **From** and **To** while everything under them — the payload
(`source_version_id`, `target_type`), the engine, the docs and every artboard — said source and
target. *From/To* also reads as direction, which is right for a relationship link and meaningless
for an anchor: `Company ≡ Stock` has two sides, not a start and an end. One pair of words now, and
it is the pair that is stored.
