Modeller is its own product.

`/u/<user>/<graph>/modeller` opens **Invana Modeller** on the Global model. Its rail reads Global
model · Models · Stitches · Requests, then Worlds · Guardrails · Rules, with Graph settings at the
bottom, and every rail item is a path, so reloading or sharing it reopens the same screen. Rules
has its own panel. Requests says it opens once the engine records requests. The graph page's own
rail no longer carries Model, Govern or Settings.
