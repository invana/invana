A token is created in a dialog, and the secret arrives in the same one (PT10).

The form stood above the list with its name field, expiry picker and button on one row —
three controls of three heights that never shared a baseline, and a form nobody asked for
sitting over the thing they came to read.

**New token** now opens a dialog with the two fields stacked, and on submit that same
dialog turns into the secret with Copy and its one-time warning. Nobody dismisses a form to
be handed the thing they opened it for. At the ceiling the button is disabled and says the
count, instead of refusing after the typing.
