An agent is one page with five tabs: **Overview · Skills & callables · Thinking · Soul · Activity** (AG23).

- **Overview** reads the agent whole — who it is, its focus, its effort, its skills, its own guardrail as a cast table, its limits and the two policy switches (*can be assigned* · *unattended*).
- **Skills & callables** is two tables that name each other: each skill's version, the callables its plan needs — marked when the envelope lacks one — and offered / applied; each callable's bound, pinned arguments and what needs it, *nothing bound* when nothing does. *Edit envelope* opens the allow-list against the whole catalogue.
- **Thinking** is the focus and the effort; an empty effort cell shows the number a run reads.
- **Soul** is the four voice dials beside the Markdown, Invana's default voice as the placeholder when nothing is written, the character count, and a preview that answers one sample ask in the current voice and in the draft.
- **Activity** draws the meters beside the limits that cap them, the runs, the sessions (the Graph-wide count, and your own), the lineage and the event feed.

Edits from every tab are one buffer: Discard · Save appear in the header while it differs, and Save sends only what moved. A refused save names the dial or key the engine refused. The *can be assigned* switch now writes the engine's `can_be_assigned` key.
