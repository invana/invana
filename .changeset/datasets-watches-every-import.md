Studio has a Datasets product.

The launcher now opens *Invana Datasets* at `/datasets` — install-wide, so the header shows no
Graph. Its rail is Catalogue · Validation · Standards │ Imports. **Imports** lists every load into
every Graph you can read (`invana records import` and `invana loader` runs), newest first, with
imports a day over the last 30 days and the command to start one; a row opens the run's page in its
Graph's Activity. Catalogue, Validation and Standards say what they will hold and that they open
when the engine records datasets and standards.
