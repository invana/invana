A graph answer waits for *Load to canvas* again.

A reply that returned a subgraph showed its subgraph card — "12 nodes · 6 edges added to the
canvas — nothing replaced" — beside the *Load to canvas* offer, before anything was loaded. The
card read the stored emission, and the engine writes every subgraph emission as on the canvas.
Whether a subgraph is on the canvas is now the reply's own fact — its click, or the thread's
`load` turn — so the card appears once the person has loaded it, and says so truthfully.
