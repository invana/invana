The world moves from the page header into the Ask Assistant's composer, and belongs to the open session (AD15–AD21).

- Every composer control — ask kind, world, query language, timeout — is the kit's inline `RichSelect`, opening upward. The world picker sits between the ask kind and the agent: *Ask in* · Everything and each world (a stale one disabled with its reason) · ☐ *Next ask only* · *Manage worlds…*.
- Picking a world sets it for the thread; with *Next ask only* the trigger is tagged for one ask and returns to the thread's world after it is sent. A new session starts in *Everything*; a world picked before the first ask becomes the new thread's.
- Each asked turn says what it ran in (`12:10 · ran in EU · H1 2026`), and a thread whose world was deleted says so.
- The thread header gains **Session settings**: the spend per run, with the agent's cap beside it.
- The canvas — restore check, expansions and the Types legend — works in the open thread's world; `?lens=` is retired.
- An agent's page and the Agents drawer show its own guardrail instead of a world it works in.
