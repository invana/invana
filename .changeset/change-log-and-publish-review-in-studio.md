The change log and the publish review in Studio. Activity › Events gains a *Changes* filter — every new version of a skill, rule, plan, agent or model, with who, why and the effect after in runs and tokens — and *Compare* opens any two versions side by side. The model page has a History tab, and a dataset record's change log names who made each version. Publishing a model now asks the engine first: an additive draft shows who reads the model and publishes, a breaking one shows what it breaks and requests approval from another owner, then follows the preview and the migration run. A model with a draft shows a Comparing bar over the global model.
