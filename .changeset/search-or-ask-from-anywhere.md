Studio has ⌘K, an Inbox, and a first run.

**⌘K** (Ctrl K off a Mac, or *Search or ask…* in the header) searches the open Graph by name —
conversations, models, worlds, agents, skills, projects, todos and runs — plus each product's
sections and the Graphs you can open, grouped by product. *Ask the assistant* puts what you typed in
the assistant's composer without sending it, so you still choose the agent and the world.

**Activity › Inbox** now lists what needs you on the Graph: runs waiting on someone's answer, and
runs that failed in the last 7 days, each with its product and its next step. The header's
**Notifications** shows how many.

**First run.** A member with no Graph is asked *What are you trying to decide?* and offered three
ways in: connect a graph database, start from data in Datasets, or open a sample Graph (airways or
arabidopsis) when the install has one.
