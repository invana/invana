Models is a stacked panel now, with no panel chrome above its drawers.

The Models rail was still drawing itself as a panel header over a stack — a header holding a breadcrumb and a row of icons, then the drawers, then in the list view a second strip of badges and buttons under that. Three bars before the first model. It is the stack itself now, like Govern, Library, Projects and Skills: the first drawer header is the top of the column, and the breadcrumb over the panel already answers *where am I*.

**Every control acts from the drawer whose list it is about.** `New model`, `Import`, `All models`, `Refresh` and `Search` sit on the **Models** header; `+ add` on **Node types** and **Edge types**; `Declare a stitch` on **Stitches**. The badge strip is gone — a count has to read while its drawer is closed, so it rides the drawer header, and the totals (`4 models · 0 stitches · 13 types derived`) read on the panel's status bar.

**Picking a model drills the first drawer in.** The header becomes `MODELS / AirRoutes` with a `‹` back beside it, and its body is what that model is: the description line, the version ladder, the compatibility banner, and the *published — open a draft* notice. The type drawers take the rest of the column. The panel takes no close control any more: the rail icon that opened it closes it.
