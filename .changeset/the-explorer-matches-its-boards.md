The Explorer is drawn as its prototype boards draw it (docs/products/explorer.md).

**The header, in every product.** The Graph is a switcher (`airways ▾`) rather than an
`owner › graph` trail, and the levels follow it: `airways ▾ › Sessions › <title>`. The header
holds search, Notifications, the Assistant and the avatar, nothing more: the theme, *Tools
always shown* and *Graph setup* are in the user menu. The repository stars, fullscreen and
set-up buttons are gone from it.

**The board.** With nothing open, the Explorer is one question box on the dotted ground, no
tabs: the person's recent questions under it, one click to ask again, and *Your views* — their
kept canvases. A Graph that cannot answer yet shows its lock and *Open the Modeller* instead.
Once a canvas is open the strip holds its tabs and *+* only. A toolbar floats top-left —
select · lasso · box, growing to the full set when the header's *Tools* switch is on, with the
legend bottom-left — and zoom in · out · fit sit bottom-right. The minimap is gone. A clicked
record opens as a card on the board, with *Ask about*, *Expand* and *Hide*; the right side stays
the chat's, so the inspector panel is gone. The footer reads the database and its version, and
the renderer with its frame rate.

**The chat.** The panel is *Assistant · <session>*, with Sessions, settings and close. A question
is a bubble; a reply is its words, what it stood on, then *Load to canvas* · *Save view* ·
*Schedule this* · *Add to a project* · *Share*, with the steps folded under *How I got this*. *Schedule this* is
new: it opens the schedule dialog filled with the question. Sessions is a view over the open
thread, newest first, and no longer closes the board. The composer drops its timeout picker and
file attachment. The console opens on Records when a query answers, and a reply's record count
opens it.

**The assistant can be switched off.** *Assistant* in the user menu hides it in every product —
the header button, the docked chat, its reopen pill, ⌘K's *Ask the assistant* and a record's
*Ask about*. The Explorer's start then keeps your views and says how to turn asking back on.
