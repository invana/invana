A Runs row leads with what was asked (SR45 · SR65).

Line one is the prompt as it was typed, the query text in monospace for a `ql-*` run, or the
dataset for a load. Line two is the short id, the plan, then what the run spent. The status word
is gone: a glyph in the leading slot says it by shape as well as colour — a check, a cross, a
spinner while running, a pause while it waits on an approval, a question when it could not
answer, an alert when it served in part, a slash when cancelled.

The journal is now one drawer. Its funnel opens a row of filter chips — `kind · status · role ·
agent · since` — each its own dropdown and clearable on its own, and the status bar says how many
of the page are shown. Panel search boxes are the 26px panel field, not the 40px page one. `GET …/runs` rows carry the run's `role`.
