The modeller draws on one canvas: *All models* and each model's own canvas are the global model
canvas, and every stitch on it is dashed (ST57 · ST19 · ME26 · GM1–GM12).

The canvas header has a **Detail** select, which draws each node type as a circle (High), a card
with its description and key (Medium), or a schema card listing every property with its type (Low).
It also has a **Layout** select (ELK or Force), Settings, and a theme toggle. Stitches between models
are dashed lines and a model's own edge types are solid. A staged stitch is dashed in green. Hover a
type for its properties, or a stitch for its rule.

On *All models*, turn on the **Stitch** tool and drag a type onto a type in another model. The
declare card opens in the right-hand panel with both sides filled in, and the two models stay in
view. Staged stitches are committed from a bar above the canvas.

On a model's canvas, **Node type** and **Edge type** on the bar above it add a type. Dragging on the
canvas no longer connects types. Zooming no longer collapses the models into tabs, and the canvas no
longer has a legend.
