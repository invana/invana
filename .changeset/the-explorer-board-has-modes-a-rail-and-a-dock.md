The Explorer board is canvas's board. Top-left is the mode picker (Explore · Edit · Annotate · Focus · Present) with each mode's tools; top-right is a rail of panels — Legend, Styling, History, Versions, Element, Changes, Export — one open at a time; bottom-left is the minimap over zoom, fit and lock. Every other verb is on the right-click menus: *Layout…* opens the preset cards where you clicked, and *Expand…* opens the fine-tune dialog. Tree, Flow and Radial lay out again, nodes are labelled by name (or by a readable id), and a model's frames keep their colours across a theme switch (GC21 · GC23 · GC24 · GC27).
