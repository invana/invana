Branches, findings, decisions and reports in Invana Projects.

- **New project** asks for the type (research · policy · general), the question, the first branch, the
  agents on it and who can see it — Graph members, or only the people on it.
- **A project opens as a page** beside the Projects list: Overview, Branches, Decisions, Lineage and
  Report. Its tab and what is drilled into survive a reload.
- **Branches** are drawn as a tree, merges as dashed edges. They fork into children that inherit the parent's findings. A branch's page shows its
  work, its findings for and against, agent proposals to accept or reject, and its decision history.
- **Findings** carry a stance and what they cite; an agent's waits for a person's *Review*.
- **Decide** records a verdict and a decision with a rationale. Every rule in force is checked; a rule
  that blocks names the findings that broke it and offers to take them out of the cites. A later
  decision supersedes, never edits. **Merge** carries chosen findings into another branch.
- **Lineage** draws a decision back through its findings to the runs and datasets they cite and the
  datasets' authors, every link named, filterable by origin.
- **Report** reads rendered, cites numbered; it drafts from the records, saves, publishes frozen versions, and exports PDF or Markdown.
- The header's breadcrumb names the project, the branch and the finding you are on.
- **Decisions** lists every decision across the Graph's projects, with *Export for audit* as CSV.
- **My todos** lists your own todos when no project is open.
- In the Explorer, **Add to a project** keeps an answer as a finding on a branch, its run cited.
- **Fix:** asking from the Graph's centred box no longer sometimes closes the assistant again as the
  session opens.
