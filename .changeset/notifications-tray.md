The bell opens a tray (docs/products/shell.md › The tray).

**Decide without leaving the page.** Notifications in the header no longer navigates: it opens a
tray over whatever is open. **Needs you** lists the approvals waiting on you and the runs waiting
on your answer; **Updates** lists your notifications, unread with a dot. *Mark all read*, *Rules*
and *Open inbox* sit around them. With nothing there it says *Nothing needs you* and offers
*New rule*.

**Review inside the tray.** *Review* opens the approval in place: exactly what will be sent, what
*Approve* and *Decline* each do — for an agent's held step, *Approve* sends it and the run carries
on, *Decline* sends nothing — who asked, the run it came from, the approvers and the time left,
with a note. The Inbox's dialog shows the same review. A member not named reads it with both acts
off and the approvers named; past the deadline it reads *expired — nothing was sent*.

**A toast for what is new.** An approval or a question that arrives while Studio is open raises a
toast; *Review* opens it in the tray. Updates never toast.

A run held on an approval now shows once, as the approval, instead of also as a run waiting on an
answer.
