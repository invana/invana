A skill opens as a page in the main area, and the Skills drawer is its list (SK17 · SK36–SK38).

Selecting a skill in the drawer shows a quick look under the list: its version, its when-to-use
sentence, offered and applied on the current version, and how many agents carry it. **Open** gives
the skill's page: its name, version and origin over five tabs, `Playbook · Flow · Bindings · Usage ·
Versions`. Editing, publishing, binding and unbinding all happen on the page, and `Usage…` in its
header opens the usage board. A new skill opens its page on Playbook, ready to write.

The page replaces the read-only skill board. Reports saved from that board still open.
