Studio's sign-in, setup and assistant copy now use the product's own words. The sign-in panel describes one model for
scattered data, answers you can trace, a model you can evolve and agents that propose while people decide — and no
longer promises game theory or parameter sweeps. The setup card, the first-run page, the assistant's view-of-the-graph
picker and its refusal card say *model*, *links between models*, *view of the graph* and *rule* in place of ontology,
stitches, worlds and guardrails.
