A paused step no longer echoes the clarifying question.

When a turn stops to ask something, the reply bubble already shows the question and its option buttons — the `needs input` step row underneath repeated it word for word, so the same question was on screen twice (three times with the pinned strip). The step row now says what it is waiting on instead (`needs input · 4 options offered`, or `needs input · waiting on your answer` when there are none), and the strip shows that same step detail. The question itself is unchanged in the reply and in the step's trace.
