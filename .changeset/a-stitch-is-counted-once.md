Each stitch is listed and counted once (ST58). A republish carries a model's stitches onto its new
version, and the old version's links used to be listed beside them — `GET …/model-links`,
`invana stitches list` and the All models chip showed every stitch twice. They now read links
between active versions only. The chip reads `— stitches` while the links load, never `0`.
