The agent's page and the Agents list are drawn as their artboards (AG36 · AG37 · AG38).

**The list.** Each row is an agent's name, its kind, its guardrail (or *Graph guardrails only*) and its spend this month. Search and `+` sit in the drawer header with the count; kind, status and *show ephemeral* are behind its filter button. Selecting a row gives a short quick look under the list — kind, *Graph default*, description, guardrail, a spend meter, runs at once and skills needing attention — with **Open** as its only act. Rows carry no actions.

**The page.** Its five tabs are the kit's underline tab strip, and every section is a bordered card with its link on the right. Overview is two columns, with the policy as two switches that still say when the Graph decides. Skills shows each skill's needs as chips, the missing ones in red, and a band per skill naming what the envelope lacks with **Edit envelope · Unbind**. Thinking sets focus beside effort; Activity's runs are a table — run, ask, outcome, cost, when — and its limits read as `max_*` rows until **Edit**. Pause and Retire are in the page's header only.

Run list rows carry the run's `outcome` beside its status.
