The models board reads as drawn, and a saved report reopens as it was kept.

- **The head** is `Models` and a scope picker — *All models* or one model — with the
  `N published` · `N stitches` chips, *Save report* · *New model* · *Import*, and *Reports*
  behind `⋯`.
- **Lists are capped by the engine.** *Needs attention* has column heads, shows six and a
  `+N more`; a model's signal shows three and `+N`; Usage's model rows count their signals
  (`5 unused types`) and *Stitches crossed* lists the five most crossed under the total.
- **Save report** keeps the open tab as a frozen reading: a banner naming who kept it and
  when, a `saved report` chip, `Reports · N`, and *Back to live*. A link to a report lands on
  the report.
- Usage's *Unused types* tile warns; Performance has an *Advice* tile; a relationship type
  several stitches share reads `N stitches` on the Database tab.
