**An agent's Overview lists its skills and your sessions with it.** A draft agent can be discarded;
*New agent* reopens your untitled draft instead of minting another. An agent's Runs page through
every run. Retire pauses the schedules that start the agent, and the Pause and Retire dialogs name
them. Fleet's month budget is the sum of the live agents' budgets, as the Agents list reads it, and
token sizes print `k` · `M` · `B`.

**A skill's History shows what the last version changed** — steps added, removed and changed — and
which agents pick it up. Flow draws a skill's steps in one column with their side cards beside
them. *Author a template* takes a kind, what it accepts and its shape, and can publish at once. A
function's page opens as `?page=function:<key>`, and `graph.read.query` declares its query required.
