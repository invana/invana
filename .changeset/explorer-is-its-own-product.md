Explorer is its own product.

`/u/<user>/<graph>/explorer` opens **Invana Explorer**: the board and the assistant, with no left
rail. The canvas tools float at the board's top-left behind a *Tools* toggle. The open session
is part of the address, `/explorer/<session>`, so reloading or sharing it reopens the thread and
its answer. The launcher lists Explorer next to Studio, and old `?panel=explorer` links land on it.
