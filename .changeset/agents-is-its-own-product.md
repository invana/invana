Agents is its own product.

`/u/<user>/<graph>/agents` opens **Invana Agents** on the agents list. Its rail reads Overview ·
Agents · Skills, then Library · Plugins · LLMs · Performance, with Settings at the bottom. Every rail
item is a path, so reloading or sharing it reopens the same screen. Library holds Plans and
Templates. The task catalogue moved to Plugins, under a registry that opens once the engine has
plugins. Overview and Performance say they open with engine support. Settings holds the Graph's
concurrency, which left the Modeller's Graph settings. Rules are no longer listed under Skills,
because they are the Modeller's. The plan page and the agent pages no longer show a cost in currency. An agent shows its token budget,
and its runs show the tokens they used. The graph page's
own rail no longer carries Library, Agents or Skills.
