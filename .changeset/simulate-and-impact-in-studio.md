Simulate and Impact in Studio. A branch — and a project, with the branch to pick — has *Simulate* whenever a simulator is installed: the dialog asks for what the simulator declares (parents, targets, progeny for genomic prediction), the dataset versions it reads, and an optional seed. The result opens over the branch, ranks every cross by the share of progeny meeting every target, and is labelled derived; a row's *Add finding* cites the simulation's run, and *Save as dataset* publishes the ranked rows as a derived dataset computed from the inputs. A branch lists its simulations in a *Simulations* tab. Every project has an *Impact* tab: pick an option and how many hops, and it draws what the records its evidence cites reach over the graph — grouped by type at each hop, the records reached most, and how sure the evidence is. Activity names *Simulation run* events.
