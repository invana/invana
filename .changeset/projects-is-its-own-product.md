Projects is its own product.

`/u/<user>/<graph>/projects` opens **Invana Projects** on the project list. Its rail reads
Projects · My todos · Decisions. A project opens at `/projects/<project>` and a todo at
`/projects/todos/<todo>`, so reloading or sharing either reopens the same screen. Decisions says
it opens with engine support. The graph page's own rail no longer carries Projects.
