Activity is one page on every Graph.

`/u/<user>/<graph>/activity/<tab>` opens Activity full width, with seven tabs: Inbox, Approvals,
Runs, Schedules, Events, Rules and Channels. Runs is the journal of every run in the Graph.
Picking a run opens its page at `/activity/runs/<run>`, and a reload lands on the same run.
Events lists every write. Inbox, Approvals, Schedules, Rules and Channels say what they will
hold and what has to exist before they fill. The bell in the header opens Activity from any
screen inside a Graph. The `Runs` and `Events` icons in the left bar open their tabs.
