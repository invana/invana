Roles and access in Studio. Graph settings gains a *Members* tab: every member with their Graph roles and since when; an owner adds a member by email or username with roles, changes their roles, or removes them, and a *Roles* key says what each role may change. The connection tab is now labelled *Database*. Every product reads what you may change on a Graph: an act whose role you lack shows disabled, saying which role it needs and who holds it, and a skill, agent, model or project you may not change opens with *You can read this … Editing needs the … role — ask …*. *New project* offers the Graph's other members as well as its agents, and a private project's header names the people on it. Activity names *Roles changed* and *Removed* member events.
