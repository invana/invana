Schedules, the Inbox, rules and channels in Studio. Activity › Schedules shows what fires in the next seven days and every schedule; *New schedule* takes plain instructions, an agent, how often and when, where to deliver, and what to do with a missed firing, and each schedule has a page with its instructions, its firings and *Run now*, *Pause* and *Edit*. The Inbox now holds the approvals waiting on you — *Review* shows exactly what will be sent before you approve or decline — the runs waiting on your answer, and what your rules and schedules told you, with unread marks and *Mark all read*; the bell counts the same. Activity › Rules says which events reach whom, in the app or on a channel, and Activity › Channels adds and removes email, Slack and webhook channels. The engine's member list now carries each member's `user_id`.
